# NOIR Salon Queue Management System — Project Brief

## Project Goal
NOIR aims to reduce customer waiting confusion and help salon staff manage queues efficiently by providing a real-time, transparent queue tracking flow across customer smartphones, reception tablets, and waiting-area TV displays.

## Problem
On weekends customers sit 1–2 hours in the salon without knowing their turn. Some leave; others argue about who came first.

## Users
Customers, receptionist/owner, barbers.

## Must-have features
1. Join the queue from your phone: pick service(s) and barber (or 'Any')
2. Live status: your position, estimated wait, 'You're next!' alert
3. Receptionist dashboard: add walk-ins, call next, mark no-show
4. Barber screen: current customer, Start / Done
5. Waiting-area TV: who's with which barber + next 5 tokens
6. Owner settings: services (time + price), barbers on duty, open/close queue

## Don't build
Online payment, future-date appointments, SMS/WhatsApp, loyalty, multiple branches.

## Look & feel
- Premium, clean, mobile-first design.
- Multilingual support: English and Gujarati.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Outer backgrounds: `#EDECEA` (warm off-white)
  - Cards and inner boxes: `#7E9A82` (sage green)
  - Text: `#432f2e` (dark brown)
